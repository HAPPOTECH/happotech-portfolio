# Surface brief: HAPPOTECH home (one-page portfolio)

Mode: Experience (the shipped work leads), with a Persuade close (contact).
Audience: company decision-makers, mostly on phones, arriving from a direct conversation. Action: open WhatsApp or e-mail.
Proof: three shipped products with real captures (`public/projets/`). No photos, no stats, no testimonials.
Pinned: layout model https://7arr67l.aura.build (glass nav, centred hero, marquee, services, work, about, process, contact footer); brand logo and its blue-to-cyan gradient.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The HAPPOTECH mark is the only light in a black room, and every shipped project is shown under it. Refuses the purple-blob AI portfolio padded with invented numbers.

OWN-WORLD: near-black ink ground; one light, the logo's royal-blue-to-cyan gradient, born at the hero mark and handed to the front project card, used as light (halo, rules, focus, key words) never as broad fills or per-object glows; service captures shown monochrome under that light, true colour on hover; glass panels with a 1px white edge and inner top highlight; Archivo expanded for display echoing the wordmark: sentence case for headings (long French headings stay legible on two lines and on phones), expanded caps kept for lettering moments (nav wordmark, design band, closing HAPPOTECH band); Geist for text, Geist Mono for small labels; pill controls, 24px cards, 16px media.

STORY: in seconds the visitor knows what HAPPOTECH builds, sees three real products, understands the method, and writes on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: floating glass nav (mark, 4 links, "Démarrer un projet"); centred H mark ~150px lit from behind by a pointer-following blue light; headline "Vos idées, nos solutions digitales." letter reveal on 2 lines; short sub (under 20 words, two lines); CTAs "Voir nos projets" + "Démarrer un projet"; availability pill fixed bottom-left on xl, shown only while the hero is on screen.

SIGNATURE: pointer light in hero; sticky-stacking project cases; scroll-drawn method line; typing code panel.

FORM: user-pinned aura.build layout (canon, pinned over the roll), raised by: one-light discipline (from the indoor-weather-sun challenger), full-measure wordmark band closing the page (from the hatch-show-print challenger). Seed key 0e42c94d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
