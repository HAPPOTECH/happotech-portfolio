## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: decision-makers at companies and organisations (mostly in Senegal, francophone) who have a problem or an idea that needs a digital answer, and who are evaluating whether HAPPOTECH can deliver. They arrive from a direct conversation (HAPPOTECH reaches out, or they were referred), often on a phone, and want to judge quickly: what do these people build, is it real, how do I reach them.

## Product Purpose

HAPPOTECH is a two-founder digital studio (Mohamed Faye and Mouhamadou Moustapha Gueye, student entrepreneurs). Companies bring their problems or ideas; HAPPOTECH turns them into concrete, adapted digital solutions. This site is the studio's portfolio: it must prove, with shipped work, that the studio delivers, and make contacting the founders effortless. Success: a visitor opens a WhatsApp chat or an e-mail.

## Positioning

Not a template shop: HAPPOTECH starts from the client's problem, not from a catalogue, and ships the whole thing (design, front, back). The proof is three real, shipped products in three different sectors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services offered (confirmed):
